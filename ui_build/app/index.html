How to use this view
  1. Pick a Tournament (or keep All tournaments). The Year list follows the selected tournament automatically.
  2. The Phase 0 leaderboard re-calculates instantly on every Tournament/Year change — no button needed for it — and always sorts by net rating, descending.
  3. To look up a player: start typing their name in Find player — matching names appear as suggestions (↑ ↓ to move, Enter or click to pick). You can also browse the full Player dropdown instead.
  4. Press Search to apply the typed name. The match log filters to that player and their leaderboard row is highlighted.
  5. Reset filters returns tournament, year, player and the suggestion box to the full dataset.
